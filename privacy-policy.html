Privacy Policy - The Brown Heart App

Last Updated: October 23, 2025

Overview

The Brown Heart App ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Collect

  • Personal Health Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal health information
  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • Location Data: We do not access or collect your location information
  • Usage Analytics: We do not track or collect data about how you use the app
  • Device Information: We do not collect device-specific information

Voice Data Processing

  • Local Processing Only: When you use voice features, speech processing occurs entirely on your device
  • No Voice Data Storage: We do not record, store, or transmit your voice data
  • No External Sharing: Voice input is never sent to external servers or third parties

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, we do not use your data for:

  • Marketing purposes
  • Analytics or tracking
  • Sharing with third parties
  • Creating user profiles

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect personal data.

Microphone Permission

Our app requests microphone access solely to provide voice input functionality for health questions. This feature:

  • Processes speech locally on your device
  • Does not record or store audio
  • Does not transmit voice data externally
  • Can be disabled in your device settings at any time

Educational Content

All health information provided by the app is:

  • For educational purposes only
  • Not a substitute for professional medical advice
  • Based on publicly available health guidelines
  • Not personalized medical recommendations

Data Security

While we do not collect personal data, we implement security measures to protect the app's functionality and ensure user privacy.

Children's Privacy

Our app is rated 4+ and contains only educational health information suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Compliance

This app is designed to comply with:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) principles

Summary

The Brown Heart App prioritizes your privacy by not collecting any personal or health data. Voice processing is done locally on your device, and all health information is educational only.
